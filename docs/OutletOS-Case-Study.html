How a six-restaurant group replaced five dashboards with one live view

An illustrative rollout of OutletOS at a group running six restaurants across four countries, from the first POS connection to every role working from one screen in four weeks.

6restaurants
4countries

The challenge

The group was growing fast, but every answer lived in a different tool: sales in two POS systems, delivery in five partner portals, rotas in spreadsheets, fridge temperatures on paper, and CCTV that nobody had time to watch.

  • Owners learnt about a bad night the next morning
  • Area managers spent hours stitching reports together
  • Hygiene and cold-chain checks depended on memory
  • Regular guests were never recognised across branches
The solution

OutletOS connected to the systems already in place, gave every role its own live workspace, and added a team of AI agents that answer questions within each role’s access.

  • POS & billing
  • Inventory & menu
  • Kitchen operations
  • Vision intelligence
  • Reports, KRIs, forecasts
  • Finance & compliance
  • AI agents & alerts
  • Role-based access
