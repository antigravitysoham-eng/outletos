OverviewHow it works

The bot is a second front door to the same OutletOS server. It never has its own copy of your data and never decides access on its own.

AnswerSame tools and AI agents as the web app
What people use it for
  • Owners: the day's numbers between meetings, the weekly report on a Monday morning.
  • Branch managers: alerts and kitchen speed during service, acknowledging from the floor.
  • Chefs: their station's ticket times, cold-chain warnings.
What it will not do
  • Answer in group chats (refused for privacy).
  • Show anything the person cannot see in the web app.
  • Change data without an explicit Approve tap.
  • Work for people whose OutletOS account is deactivated.

Part A · AdministratorsSet up the bot (10 minutes)

Done once by the owner or IT administrator (anyone with the integrations.manage permission).

  1. Create the bot in TelegramOpen a chat with @BotFather (the official bot with the blue tick). Send /newbot, choose a display name (for example “Spice Route Ops”) and a username ending in bot (for example spiceroute_ops_bot). BotFather replies with a token like 123456789:AAH…. Treat it like a password.
    Optional polish in BotFather: /setuserpic for your logo, /setdescription for the text people see before they press Start, /setjoingroups → Disable so the bot cannot be added to groups.
  2. Connect it in OutletOSGo to Administration → Integrations → Telegram assistant. Paste the token, choose how messages arrive (next step) and click Connect bot. OutletOS checks the token with Telegram, registers the command menu and shows Live · @yourbot.
  3. Choose long polling or webhook
    ModeUse whenWhat you need
    Long polling (default)One server, pilots, servers behind a firewall or on a private networkOutbound internet access to api.telegram.org. Nothing to open inbound.
    WebhookProduction, several servers behind a load balancerA public HTTPS address for OutletOS (for example https://ops.yourcompany.com). OutletOS registers /api/telegram/webhook/<secret> with a second secret header, so forged calls are rejected.
  4. Tell your teamAsk people to click Telegram in the OutletOS top bar and follow the three steps (Part B). They appear under Linked people as they connect.

Day-to-day administration

TaskHow
Disconnect a personIntegrations → Telegram assistant → the unlink icon next to their name. Deactivating their OutletOS account also disconnects them automatically on their next message.
Pause the botPause stops answering everyone; links are kept. Save & reconnect resumes.
Rotate the tokenIn BotFather send /revoke, pick the bot, then paste the new token and click Save & reconnect. Links survive the rotation.
See usageThe card shows requests in the last 24 hours and each person's last activity. Every command is written to the telegram_log table; AI answers are also in the agent run log.

Part B · EveryoneLink your chat (2 minutes)

  1. Open the Telegram windowClick Telegram in the OutletOS top bar. (No button? Your administrator has not switched the bot on yet.)
  2. Open the botTap Open @yourbot: on a phone it opens Telegram straight into the chat. Tap Start.
  3. Or send the codeOn another device, send /start ABCD2345 with the code shown. Codes work once and expire after 10 minutes.
  4. DoneThe window turns green and the bot greets you with your name, role and the commands you can use.
Change phones or Telegram accounts? Click Disconnect Telegram in the same window, then link again.

The IT / Integrations role sets the bot up but has no operational data access, so it only gets /help, /whoami and /unlink.

Approvals

The bot never changes data on its own. When you ask for /alerts (or ask the agent to clear alerts), it shows ✅ Acknowledge and Dismiss buttons. Tapping Acknowledge checks your permission again, acknowledges only alerts you may see, records “Your name (Telegram)” on each alert and confirms how many were cleared. Buttons expire after 30 minutes and only work in the chat they were sent to.

Push alerts

High-severity alerts (for example a walk-in door left open, a freezer out of range, a station unattended) are pushed to linked people who can see that alert, with an Acknowledge button. At most 12 pushes per person per hour. Send /notify off to stop them, /notify on to resume.

TrustSecurity and privacy

ControlHow it works
IdentityA chat is linked to one OutletOS user with a single-use code that expires in 10 minutes. Codes are 8 characters from a 32-character alphabet (about 1012 combinations).
AuthorisationEvery message re-loads the user and their role. Permissions, branch scope and station filters are the same code paths as the web app and API.
DeactivationA deactivated user's chat is disconnected on its next message and receives nothing further.
Private chats onlyGroup and channel messages are refused, so numbers never land in a shared group by accident.
TokenStored on the OutletOS server, never returned to the browser in full (shown masked). Rotate with BotFather /revoke.
Webhook protectionA secret URL path and Telegram's secret-token header are both checked with constant-time comparison.
Abuse limits20 messages per minute per chat; 12 push alerts per hour per chat.
AuditEvery command, link, unlink and approval is logged with time, chat and user.
Know what leaves your server. Bot messages travel through Telegram's servers and, unlike Telegram secret chats, are not end-to-end encrypted. Answers contain the same business figures people see in the app (sales, alerts, rosters). Pay rates and guest phone numbers are only included for roles allowed to see them in OutletOS. If your policy forbids business data on third-party messengers, leave the bot switched off: OutletOS works fully without it.

HelpTroubleshooting

SymptomCause and fix
No Telegram button in the top barThe bot is not connected or is paused. An administrator connects it in Integrations.
“That link code is invalid or has expired”Codes work once, for 10 minutes. Open the Telegram window again for a fresh code.
“This chat isn't linked”The chat was disconnected (by you, an administrator, or because your account was deactivated). Link again.
“Your role doesn't include /sales”Working as designed: the command needs a permission your role does not have. Ask your owner if you need it.
Bot does not answer at allCheck the card on Integrations for Last error. In polling mode the server needs outbound access to api.telegram.org; in webhook mode the public address must be HTTPS and reachable.
“For privacy OutletOS only answers in a private chat”You messaged it from a group. Open a direct chat with the bot.
